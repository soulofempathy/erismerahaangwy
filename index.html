You became one of the few places my mind goes when it seeks quiet.

Act VII — The Ordinary & Infinite

In The Midst of It All

The shared laughter, the small inside jokes only we know the roots of.

I find myself noticing every ordinary detail when it touches you: coffee cups left half-warm, quiet walks, the late hours when neither of us wants to say goodnight.

Act VIII — Nocturne

The night holds no color that belongs to pride,

Only the quiet tide where memories hide.

I find you in the hush before the dawn starts to break,

A promise kept for nobody else's sake.

Act IX — Constancy

Whatever shadows arrive, whatever distance stretches between our hands:

I am not going anywhere.

If I only had one thing to leave behind in this life,

it would be the absolute certainty that you were loved with patience, with reverence, and with everything I had.

Yours, always,
